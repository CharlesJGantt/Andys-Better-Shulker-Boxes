# Andy's Better Shulker Boxes 1.1.7

This update makes item labels persistent, recoverable, and placeable on the shulker-box face you choose.

- Item labels no longer disappear when their chunk unloads.
- Labels now appear on the clicked top, bottom, north, south, east, or west face, including the outward-facing lid of wall- and ceiling-placed shulkers.
- Removing, replacing, or destroying a label safely returns the original label item or drops it nearby.
- Mining a labeled shulker keeps the label attached to the shulker item, whether the box is empty or full, and restores it when the box is placed again.
- Names, lore, enchantments, durability damage, and other stored item data remain intact.
- Restored labels stay hidden until their correct icon is ready, preventing a brief paper fallback.

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.120 or newer with both included packs active. No cheats or experiments are required.
